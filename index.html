Privacy Policy – Etherlume

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Etherlume ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our Pinterest integration and related services.

1. Who we are

Etherlume is operated by [Etherlume], based in [Berchtesgaden, Germany]. For any privacy-related questions, contact us at [business.aipic@gmail.com].

2. What data we access via the Pinterest API

Our application connects to the Pinterest API solely to manage our own Pinterest business account. Through this connection we may:

  • Read data about our own Pins and Boards (titles, descriptions, IDs, basic performance metrics such as impressions, saves, and clicks)
  • Create new Pins on our own Boards

We do not access, read, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user or account.

3. How we use this data

Data retrieved from the Pinterest API is used exclusively to:

  • Automate the publishing of our own content to our own Pinterest Boards
  • Track the performance of our own Pins for internal content planning

4. Data storage and sharing

Access tokens and credentials are stored securely in private, access-controlled environments and are never shared with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or trade any data obtained through the Pinterest API. Performance data may be stored internally in private project-management tools (e.g. Notion) accessible only to us.

5. Data retention

We retain Pin performance data only as long as necessary for our own content planning purposes and delete it when no longer needed.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we may hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at [business.aipic@gmail.com].

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to [business.aipic@gmail.com].
